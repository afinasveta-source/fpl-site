For Game participants

Review by a judge

A league judge will look at your work and record an audio review of about five minutes: what worked, what to improve and how — with technical details. Reviews are in Russian.

Reviews open after a Game ends, for 30 days. You cannot order right now — come back after the final.

What to review

We will send the invoice here. Best of all — the email you use for your league account: the review will appear there.

Terms

  1. The review is an audio recording of about 5 minutes by a league judge (Milena), in Russian. It is the expert's personal opinion and does not affect round results, rating or certificates.
  2. Orders open after the Game ends, for 30 days. One work — 300 ₽, all your works of the Game — 700 ₽.
  3. After you order, the organizer will send an invoice to your contact within one working day.
  4. The review will be ready within 3 days after payment. Listen in your account, section “My reviews”; if you have not signed in yet, we will send you a sign-in link.
  5. If we miss the deadline, we refund in full on request. Once the review is delivered, payment is not refunded.
  6. Your name and contact are used only for this order.
How it works

1. After the Game ends, you order here. 2. You get an invoice. 3. The judge looks at your works and records the review. 4. You listen in your account whenever you like, as often as you like.
